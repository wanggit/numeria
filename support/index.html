Technical support

How can we help?

Numeria is an offline calculator. Your calculations stay on your iPhone, so most issues can be solved without an account or a network connection.

Quick check: update Numeria from the App Store, then close and reopen the app. If a result still looks wrong, send us the calculator name, inputs, and the expected result.

Common questions

Why is my result different?

Check the selected frequency, compounding method, tax year, units, and rounding. Financial calculators can produce different answers when any of these assumptions differ.

Does Numeria need internet?

No. Calculations run on-device. Internet is only needed for App Store downloads and updates.

Where is my data stored?

Only small preferences, such as recently used settings, may be stored locally on your device. Numeria does not upload them.

Is this financial advice?

No. Numeria provides estimates for learning and planning. Confirm important decisions with a qualified professional and current official rates.

中文技术支持

Numeria 是一款离线金融计算器。计算过程在 iPhone 本地完成，不需要注册账号，也不会上传你的输入。

快速排查：请先从 App Store 更新 Numeria，然后完全关闭并重新打开 App。如果结果仍然不符合预期，请提供计算器名称、输入值和你期望的结果。

为什么我的结果不一样？

请检查计息频率、复利方式、税务年度、单位和四舍五入设置。只要假设不同，金融计算结果就可能不同。

需要联网吗？

不需要。所有计算都在设备上完成；只有从 App Store 下载或更新时才需要网络。

我的数据存在哪里？

App 可能只在设备本地保存最近使用的设置等少量偏好，不会将这些数据上传。

这是投资建议吗？

不是。Numeria 只提供学习和规划用的估算。重要决定请咨询专业人士，并以最新官方数据为准。

反馈问题时请提供

  1. iPhone 型号和 iOS 版本
  2. Numeria 版本（设置 → Numeria）
  3. 计算器名称和完整输入值
  4. 预期结果与实际结果
